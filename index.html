evveltimes-upload

evveltimes-upload is a small personal tool used by the owner of the Evvel Times YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, thumbnails and translations through the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service. It only acts on the channel of the Google account that authorizes it, and only when the owner runs it.
